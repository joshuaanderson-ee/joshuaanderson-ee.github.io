PROJECT STATUS: PLANNED

DC Power Supply Testing and Analysis

An electrical testing project exploring voltage regulation, current measurement, power calculations, and the performance of low-voltage DC circuits.

Circuit photos and test results coming soon

1. Project Objective

Evaluate the performance of a low-voltage DC power regulator by measuring output voltage and current under different loads.

2. Components and Equipment

Planned equipment: low-voltage DC supply, DC regulator module, digital multimeter, resistive loads, breadboard or suitable terminal connections, and jumper wires.

3. Circuit Design

Develop a test circuit with a DC regulator and several resistive loads. Document the wiring diagram and expected values.

4. Testing Procedure

Measure output voltage under different load conditions, calculate output power, and evaluate voltage regulation.

5. Results and Analysis

Record measured voltage and current values in tables, calculate power, and compare actual performance with theoretical expectations.

6. Challenges and Solutions

Document troubleshooting, measurement errors, regulator limitations, and design improvements.

7. What I Learned

Reflect on electrical measurements, DC power systems, circuit testing, and technical data analysis.
