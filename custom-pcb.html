PROJECT STATUS: PLANNED

Custom Printed Circuit Board Design

A PCB design project using KiCad to create electrical schematics, select components, and design a custom printed circuit board.

PCB design images coming soon

1. Project Objective

Learn the complete PCB design process, including schematic capture, component selection, footprint assignment, trace routing, and design verification.

2. Software and Equipment

Planned tools: KiCad, computer, electronic component datasheets, and optionally a manufactured PCB for physical testing.

3. Schematic Design

Design a simple electronic circuit, assign appropriate component symbols, and verify electrical connections. Schematics will be added here.

4. PCB Layout and Routing

Transfer the schematic into a PCB layout, arrange components, route copper traces, and run electrical and design-rule checks.

5. Testing and Verification

Document design-rule check results, 3D board previews, and any physical testing performed after manufacturing.

6. Challenges and Solutions

Record challenges involving component placement, routing, footprints, and design revisions.

7. What I Learned

Reflect on schematic design, PCB layout, technical documentation, and electrical design verification.
